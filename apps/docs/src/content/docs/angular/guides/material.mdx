[`TelixonPhoneInput`](/angular/phone-input/) shares one `<input>` with `matInput`. The picker goes
into the form field as a text prefix. Material's floating label, outline notch, and error state
follow the field without extra code.

## Live demo

Pick the United Kingdom and type `2071838750`. The label floats and the field shows `20 7183 8750`,
while the control holds `+442071838750`. Leave the field after `2071` and Material shows the error.

<AngularMaterialDemo />

## The code

The demo runs the component and the stylesheet shown below. In the component, `matTextPrefix`
places the picker on the text line of the form field. `anchor=".mdc-text-field"` names the box
Material draws around the field. The list lines up with that box. The `telixonPhone` check comes
before the `required` check, for the reason
[Build a phone field](/angular/guides/phone-field/#show-the-errors) gives. The stylesheet carries
the Material theme, the flags, and one rule that removes the trigger's border inside the form field.
That rule belongs in the global styles, because component styles do not reach the picker.
